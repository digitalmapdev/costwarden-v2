FAQ

Questions finance teams ask before they upload anything.

What data can CostWarden analyze?+

Business spending information such as invoices, supplier records and standard spend exports from accounting or ERP systems.

Do we need to replace our ERP?+

No. The current proposition is built around understanding existing spending information rather than a rip-and-replace implementation.

Does CostWarden automatically cut costs?+

No. It surfaces areas worth reviewing and helps make the evidence easier to inspect. Your team keeps control of the commercial decision.

How quickly can we see something useful?+

That depends on the scope and quality of the data. A demo is the right place to establish a realistic timeline for your setup.
